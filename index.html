1. Information We Collect

a. Account & Authentication Information

When you sign in to MindFort with Google Sign-In (using Android Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication), we collect:

  • Your unique Firebase User ID (UID)
  • Your email address
  • Your display name and profile image URL (if provided by your Google account)

b. Habit-Building, Journaling & Coaching Data

To power habit tracking, progress streaks, and personalized coaching across sessions and devices, we store the content you choose to record in MindFort:

  • Habit streaks, daily check-in records, completion timestamps, and milestone progress
  • Private journal entries, mood logs, and reflection notes
  • In-app companion progression and custom app settings (such as light/dark theme preference)
  • Messages and prompts you submit to the in-app AI habit coach

c. Subscription & Billing Metadata

All paid subscriptions (MindFort Pro) are billed and managed solely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card numbers, billing addresses, or payment credentials. To verify and restore your Pro entitlement, our backend stores only Google Play purchase tokens, subscription product IDs, base plan/offer identifiers, and entitlement status (active, trial, grace period, or expired).

d. Advertising & Consent Data (Free Tier Only)

For free-tier users, MindFort displays ads using Google AdMob and manages regional privacy consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on your consent choices, AdMob may collect your Android Advertising ID, IP address, coarse location, device model, and ad interaction metrics for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention. Subscribers to MindFort Pro do not see ads.

e. App Integrity & Security Diagnostics

MindFort uses Firebase App Check to verify that requests to our backend and AI coaching services originate from authentic installations of MindFort, helping prevent abuse and unauthorized access.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Core Habit Tracking & Sync: Save and synchronize your streaks, check-ins, journals, and milestones securely across your devices.
  • Personalized Coaching: Process your coaching prompts via Firebase AI Logic to deliver supportive habit-building and urge-management guidance.
  • Subscription Fulfillment: Verify active Google Play subscriptions, unlock MindFort Pro features, and restore purchases when you switch or reinstall devices.
  • Advertising & Consent Compliance: Serve free-tier advertisements while honoring your privacy choices under GDPR, UK GDPR, and CCPA/CPRA via Google UMP.
  • Security & Abuse Prevention: Protect user accounts and backend APIs using Firebase Authentication, per-user Firestore security rules, and Firebase App Check.

We never sell your personal information, habit records, or journal entries.

4. Device Permissions & Accessibility Service Disclosure

Prominent Disclosure — Android Accessibility Service (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE): MindFort includes an optional Content Shield feature designed to help you stay on track with your habit goals. When you explicitly enable this feature, MindFort uses Android's Accessibility Service API strictly on-device to detect explicit content or user-configured trigger keywords in other apps and browsers so it can display a protective blur and coaching intervention screen.

Strict On-Device Privacy Commitment: MindFort does NOT collect, record, log, transmit off-device, or share any screen text, keystrokes, browsing history, private messages, or Accessibility Service data with Fort Tech or any third party. All detection runs locally on your device, is optional, and can be turned off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility.

Additional Android permissions used by MindFort:

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Displays the protective blur and coaching intervention overlay when Content Shield triggers.
  • Foreground Service & Notifications (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Keeps Content Shield active when enabled and delivers habit reminders and check-in alerts.
  • Internet & Boot Completed (INTERNET, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Syncs account data with Firebase, validates Google Play subscriptions, and restarts active shield protection after a device reboot if enabled.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob on the free tier subject to your consent preferences.

5. Third-Party Services & SDKs

MindFort relies on trusted Google and Firebase infrastructure. These providers process data under their own privacy policies and security standards:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) — Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Firebase App Check, and Firebase AI Logic: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing — Account sign-in, in-app subscription billing, and purchase verification: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP) — Ad serving and privacy consent management for free-tier users: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

6. Data Security & Retention

  • Encryption in Transit: All network communication between MindFort and Firebase/Google Play servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access Controls: Cloud Firestore is protected by strict, authenticated per-user security rules so only your signed-in account can access your personal habit and journal data.
  • Retention: We retain your account and habit records only while your account remains active or until you request deletion. Local preferences stored via Jetpack DataStore remain on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall MindFort.

7. Your Privacy Rights & Account / Data Deletion

Depending on your location (including the EEA, UK under GDPR, and California under CCPA/CPRA), you have the right to:

  • Access & Portability: Request a copy of the personal data associated with your MindFort account.
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate habit records or profile details directly in the app or by contacting us.
  • Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your MindFort account and all associated cloud data.
  • Withdraw Consent & Manage Ads: Revoke optional permissions (Accessibility, Overlay, Notifications) in Android Settings at any time, or reset/limit your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

How to Delete Your MindFort Account & Data: To permanently delete your account and all cloud-stored data (profile information, streaks, daily check-ins, journal entries, and subscription records), send an email to shahaliakbar2007@gmail.com from the email address linked to your MindFort account with the subject line "MindFort Account Deletion Request". We will verify your request and permanently delete your account and associated data within 30 days. Please note that active Google Play subscriptions must be canceled directly in Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.

8. Children's Privacy

If you believe a child has provided personal data through MindFort, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
